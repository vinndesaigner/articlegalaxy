Vincent "kiooOway." Lie Ginansyah (born February 13, 2009) is an Indonesian Mobile Legends: Bang Bang player currently playing as a Gold Laner for Syndicate Reign. Hailing from Cilacap, Indonesia, kiooOway. is known within his circle for his consistent farming pattern and hypercarry hero pool, most notably on marksman heroes such as Granger, Claude, Moskov, Natan, and Karrie.

History

kiooOway. began playing Mobile Legends: Bang Bang in the Gold Laner role, gradually specializing in hypercarry marksman heroes. He currently competes under the squad name Syndicate Reign, primarily in casual and community-level matches ("mabar").
